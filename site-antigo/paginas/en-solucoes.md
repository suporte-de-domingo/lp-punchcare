# Tooling Solutions for Tablet Compression

Specialists in performance, durability, and precision.

## Services

We provide technical services that extend the lifespan of your tooling and ensure maximum efficiency in pharmaceutical production.

### Technical Polishing of Punches and Dies

We enhance surface finishing, preventing issues such as sticking and capping, and avoiding jamming.

### Dimensional Sizing and Control

We perform high-precision measurements that ensure uniformity among punches, reducing weight and hardness variations in tablets.

### Technical Consulting and Training

We train teams and provide customized diagnostics to improve compression processes and tooling usage.

### Commercial Representation of Global Brands

We are the official representatives of Adamus S.A., the largest manufacturer of punches, dies, and die segments for the pharmaceutical industry in Poland and one of the leading suppliers worldwide.

## Applications / Supported Equipment

Our solutions are applied across a wide range of equipment and components in the pharmaceutical and nutraceutical industries.

01

### Punches and Dies

Reconditioning and supply for all types and brands of tablet presses.

02

### Tablet Press Turrets and Rotors

Adjustments and consulting to achieve greater stability and efficiency.

03

### Auxiliary Equipment

Cabinets for punchs, polishing machines, dimensional measuring devices, and ultrasonic washers.

04

### Technical Parts for Tablet Presses

We support requirements for specific parts such as extraction cams, filling cams, and compression rollers.
