## 5 signs it’s time to review your punches and dies

Is production stopping more often than usual? Are tablets showing defects or sticking to the punches? These may be clear signs that your tooling needs attention. Check the most common indicators: Recurrent sticking in specific batches Tablet weight variation Formation of burrs or chipping on edges Increased frequency of tablet press downtime Visible wear or lack of technical polishing Preventive maintenance can avoid significant losses and extend the lifespan of your equipmen
