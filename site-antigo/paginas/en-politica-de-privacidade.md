# Privacy Policy

Punch Care Ltda. values transparency and respect for its users. This Privacy Policy aims to clarify how we handle personal data collected through our website.

###### 1. Collection of Information

We collect personal data voluntarily through contact forms, quotation requests, or material downloads. The information may include name, email, phone number, company, job title, and other data relevant to providing our services.

​

###### 2. Use of Information

The data provided will be used exclusively for:

- Contact the user

- Send quotations and requested materials

- Personalize customer service

- Share news and informational content, when authorized

###### 3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to third parties, except when required to comply with legal obligations or upon the explicit request of the data subject.

​

###### 4. Information Security

We adopt technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or destruction.

​

###### 5. Cookies

This website may use cookies to improve navigation and personalize the user experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings if you wish.

​

###### 6. Data Subject Rights

In accordance with the LGPD, you have the right to:

​

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data

- Withdraw consent for the use of your data

- Request data portability

To exercise these rights, please contact us at: [punchcare@punchcare.com.br](mailto:punchcare@punchcare.com.br)

​

###### 7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated at any time. We recommend that users review this page periodically to stay informed of any changes.
